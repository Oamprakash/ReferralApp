# Play Store listing (draft)

**App name** (max 30): WarmIntro – Job Referrals

**Short description** (max 80):
Get referred to IT jobs by verified employees who actually work there.

**Full description** (max 4000):

Referrals get you interviews. Cold applications mostly don't. But asking strangers for a referral is awkward, and most "hiring" posts online come from recruiters, not people who work at the company.

This app works differently: every opening is posted by a verified employee of that company.

FOR JOB SEEKERS
• Real openings only. Posters verify their work email, and every post links to the official careers page or job ID.
• Always fresh. Posts must be re-confirmed every 14 days or they disappear.
• Best matches. Upload your resume and your profile fills itself in; open roles are ranked by your skills and experience.
• Know where you stand. Track every application: shortlisted, referred, interview, offer. Get a notification at each step.
• Fair for everyone. Each post takes a limited number of applicants, so referrers actually review each one.
• Always free. No one can charge you for a referral. Report anyone who asks and the post is hidden immediately.

FOR EMPLOYEES WHO WANT TO REFER
• Verify once with your work email (lasts 90 days). It's never shown to job seekers.
• Post openings at your company in a minute, with optional screening questions.
• Applicants arrive ranked by match. Shortlist, refer and update outcomes in a few taps.
• Build your record: referrals, interviews and offers show on your posts.

PRIVACY
• Your resume is visible only to the employees whose jobs you apply to.
• Your contact details are shared only after a referrer shortlists you.
• Download a copy of your data or delete your account anytime from your profile.

Starting with IT companies in Chennai, and growing city by city.
