Privacy Policy

WarmIntro · Last updated 30 October 2026

WarmIntro connects job seekers with verified employees who post openings at their own company and can refer applicants. This policy explains what we collect, why, who sees it, and how you can download or delete it. It applies to the WarmIntro Android app.

Contact for anything in this policy: support@example.com (replace before publishing).

What we collect

DataWhy
Google account name and emailTo sign you in. You sign in with Google, which gives us your name and email only, never your password.
Profile: name, city, years of experience, skillsShown to employees whose jobs you apply to, and used to rank jobs that match you.
Resume (PDF)Shown only to employees whose jobs you apply to. When you upload it, the app reads the text on your phone to fill in your profile; the text itself is not sent to us.
Contact email and mobile for referrals (optional)Shown to an employee only after they shortlist your application, so they can fill in their company's referral form.
Applications and answers to screening questionsSent to the employee who posted the job, and used to show you the status of each application.
Work email (employees who post jobs)To confirm you work at the company. It is never shown to job seekers. Verification lasts 90 days.
Job postsShown to all signed-in users while the post is open.
Reports you fileReviewed by our moderators to keep the app safe. The reported person isn't told who reported them.
Notification token and notificationsTo send updates about your applications and posts.

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or files other than the resume you choose. We don't show ads, and we don't sell or rent your data.

Who we share it with

  • Employees you apply to: your profile, resume and answers; your contact details only after they shortlist you.
  • Service providers that run the app for us, under their own security and privacy terms: Supabase (database, file storage, sign-in), Google Firebase Cloud Messaging (push notifications), Google (sign-in with Google) and Resend (work-email verification codes).
  • Authorities, only when the law requires it.

How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile, resume, posts and applications: until you delete your account.
  • Sign-in and verification codes: deleted within a day.
  • Notifications: deleted after 180 days.
  • When you delete your account, your data is removed immediately. Copies in encrypted backups at our hosting provider expire on their normal schedule.

Your rights and choices

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and similar laws, you can access, correct and erase your data, and raise a grievance.

  • See or download your data: Profile → Download my data (a JSON file).
  • Correct it: edit your profile at any time.
  • Delete your account: Profile → Delete my account, or follow these steps if you can't use the app.
  • Notifications: turn them off in your phone's settings.
  • Grievances: write to support@example.com. We reply within 7 days.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Access is restricted per user in our database, so people only see what this policy describes. Resumes are stored privately and opened through short-lived links.

Age

WarmIntro is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone younger. If you believe a minor has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
