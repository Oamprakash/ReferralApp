Delete your WarmIntro account

And everything stored with it

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open WarmIntro and go to the Profile tab.
  2. Under Your data, tap Delete my account.
  3. Type DELETE and confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately. Want a copy first? Tap Download my data before deleting.

Without the app

Email oamprakash+warmintro@gmail.com from the Google account email you signed in with. We'll confirm it's your account and delete it within 7 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in, profile, resume and contact details
  • Your applications and screening answers
  • Your job posts (people who applied to your open posts are told the post closed) and work-email verification
  • Your notifications and notification tokens

What we keep

Nothing linked to you. Reports you filed are removed with your account. Copies in our hosting provider's encrypted backups expire on their normal schedule.
